Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Rafa Mentality ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit our websites and funnels (including start.rafamentality.com), watch our videos, submit an application, or book a call with us.

Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: name, email address, phone number, and any answers you give in our application or booking forms.
  • Automatically collected: IP address, browser and device information, pages viewed, referring URLs, and interactions with our pages and ads.
  • Cookies and tracking: we use cookies and similar technologies to operate the site, remember your preferences, and measure marketing performance.

How We Use Your Information

  • To contact you about your application, schedule and conduct calls, and provide our programs and services.
  • To send you information, updates, and marketing communications (you can opt out at any time).
  • To measure, attribute, and improve our advertising and website performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud or misuse.

Advertising and Analytics Technologies

We work with third-party platforms to deliver and measure advertising. These may include:

  • Meta Pixel & Conversions API (Meta Platforms, Inc.): we share certain events (such as page views, registrations, applications submitted, and calls booked) with Meta, including via server-side Conversions API, to measure and optimize our ads. Data may be matched to your Facebook/Instagram account. See Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Hyros: ad attribution and analytics.
  • Google: analytics and advertising measurement.
  • GoHighLevel: our CRM, forms, scheduling, and communications.

You can control cookies through your browser settings and manage ad personalization through your Meta ad preferences and Google ad settings.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate our business (such as the platforms listed above), and when required by law or to protect our rights.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide our services and for legitimate business or legal purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information, and to opt out of marketing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in each message, and reply STOP to opt out of text messages.

Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date reflects the most recent version.
